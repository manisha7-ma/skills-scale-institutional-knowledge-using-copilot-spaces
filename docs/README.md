# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This README is the entry point to OctoAcme’s project management guidance. OctoAcme moves work through **initiation, planning, execution, release, and close/retrospective**, with each stage supported by lightweight artifacts and clear decision points. The approach emphasizes customer value, iterative delivery, clear ownership, and measurable outcomes.

During **initiation**, the team validates the business need, identifies stakeholders, defines success measures, and records an initial timeline, risks, and resource needs. Once an initiative is approved, **planning** turns it into a prioritized, estimated backlog with acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, assigned responsibilities, milestones, and identified dependencies.

In **execution**, teams deliver small increments using a project board, regular check-ins, demos, and status updates. The PM coordinates delivery and communication; the Product Manager/Product Lead owns outcomes and priorities; developers build and test; QA validates acceptance; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Risks and dependencies are tracked and escalated through agreed channels so blockers and decisions remain visible.

**Release** requires acceptance criteria to be met, CI and security checks to pass, release notes and rollback plans to be ready, and smoke tests and post-deployment verification to be completed. After a sprint, release, milestone, or incident, the team holds a retrospective and tracks a small number of owned improvement actions. Across the lifecycle, quality checks, regular communication, risk management, and continuous improvement help keep delivery reliable and transparent.
